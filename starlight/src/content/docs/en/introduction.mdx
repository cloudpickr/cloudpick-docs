CloudPick is a **vendor-neutral guide for making the right decisions in multi-cloud environments**.

We do not recommend multi-cloud as a default choice. Single-vendor, hybrid, and multi-cloud strategies each carry their own costs and responsibilities, and the right choice depends on your workloads and organizational context. We explain why on-premises and network-segregation-centered operations were once necessary, and how control mechanisms can be extended in modern environments.

Each vendor's official documentation only covers its own services. CloudPick places the major global cloud vendors side by side under the same criteria, helping you decide "what should I choose in this situation?" All content is grounded in official vendor documentation and industry standards (NIST, CNCF, FinOps Foundation), with personal opinions and speculation excluded.

Whether you are new to cloud or designing multi-cloud architectures, we provide reading paths tailored to your level and purpose.

:::note
All technical content is based on official vendor documentation or industry-standard sources. When adopting or operating in production, always cross-check the latest official documentation from each vendor.
:::

## Documentation Principles

> This is CloudPick's first principle. All contributors follow it.

- **Grounded in official documentation** — All technical content is based on official vendor documentation or industry-standard sources (NIST, CNCF, FinOps Foundation, etc.). Speculative content is excluded.
- **Neutrality** — Vendor comparisons are expressed as neutrally as possible.
- **Sourcing** — Links to official documentation are collected in the **References** section at the bottom of each document.
- **Managing volatility** — Fast-changing information such as pricing, region counts, and GA/Preview status is pointed to via official links rather than stated as fixed figures. Each document notes `Last reviewed: Month YYYY` at the top.
- **Terminology conventions** — These documents follow Korean-language source conventions for the original CloudPick content: Korean is used as the primary script, with English glossed alongside technical terms or abbreviations on first use (e.g., 가용영역(Availability Zone)). Standard transliteration rules are followed (e.g., '메시지 큐' is correct, '메세지 큐' is not). Vendor names use their current official names (e.g., Microsoft Entra ID). The authoritative terms follow the [glossary](../glossary/).
